## Why this phase matters

GPT-5.6 made a material practical difference: it took a growing collection of
binary experiments and focused the current backend on shared stages, explicit
evidence, and repeatable handoff rather than a proliferation of family-specific
scripts. That consolidation is the reason the current directory can be run,
audited, packaged, and continued by another contributor.

## Consolidated results

- **One shared terminal route.** Terminal behavior belongs in
  [`src/proteusgen/component_terminal_placer.py`](src/proteusgen/component_terminal_placer.py),
  with the component catalogue/profile as the source of family facts. New
  experiment runners call that route; they do not contain terminal geometry or
  binary-link logic of their own.
- **Native attachment mechanics.** The active contract requires a grid-aligned
  terminal contact, 1800 orientation on left pins, 0 orientation on right
  pins, a nonzero donor-proven short WIRE to the pin, and rebased active
  terminal/component-link suffixes from final `ROOT.DSN` addresses.
- **Scale and mixed validation research.** Dated scale, mixed, loader-gate,
  donor-comparison, and Ctrl+S evidence is retained under
  [`../experiments/runs`](../experiments/runs), including rejected routes.
  This documentation does not promote an unaccepted IC total-mix result.
- **Value/properties editor.** The shared post-terminal editor adds a
  conservative, family-safe path for supported value/property changes. Its
  preflight evidence is in
  [`knowledge/value_and_properties_editor_preflight_2026_07_16.md`](knowledge/value_and_properties_editor_preflight_2026_07_16.md).
- **Portable executable.** [`release/ProgenProteus.exe`](release/ProgenProteus.exe)
  packages the current pipeline with its runtime donor/registry closure and
  preserves the public `progen-proteus` command shape.
- **Repository continuity.** The active runtime, experiments, archive, and
  hash-backed inventory now have explicit boundaries. This is a major
  maintainability improvement over a flat, mixed collection of current code,
  donors, one-off outputs, and recovered artifacts.

## Accuracy boundary

The GPT-5.6 consolidation is deliberately evidence-first. It does not turn a
loader-open or static assertion into a claim that a schematic is visually or
simulation accepted. The local Proteus normal-open/cold-reopen gate and user
visual review remain mandatory. The last explicitly trusted terminal-placement
checkpoint is `RESISTOR/v3` at `a6deb648`; later family work is retained as
research or candidate evidence until independently accepted.

## Continuation rule

Future progress should extend the shared catalogue, shared terminal placer,
focused tests, and donor evidence together. Do not replace an accepted family
route to make a new family pass, and do not create a separate terminal script.
