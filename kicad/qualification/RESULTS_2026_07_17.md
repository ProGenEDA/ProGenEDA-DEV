# KiCad 400-Circuit Qualification Results

## Scope

The practical corpus is 40 canonical electrical archetypes times ten named
deployment/layout profiles: 400 deterministic canonical main JSON inputs. It
is deliberately not presented as 400 unrelated circuit theories. The v2 corpus
contains 17,890 component instances, 13,490 expected nets, 116 supported
component kinds, and no circuit larger than 89 placed components.

Corpus:

```text
kicad/qualification/corpora/2026_07_17_common_400_v2/
```

## Immutable Batch Evidence

The untouched portable-executable batch is preserved at:

```text
kicad/examples/progen_kicad_executable_run_2026_07_17_184041_common_400_portable_qualification_v2/
```

Results from that run:

- 400 of 400 canonical inputs passed the deterministic input fixer.
- 400 of 400 generated projects had valid static files, values, references,
  pin resolution, expected-net comparison, wire geometry, strict-wire
  contracts, and no partial or unrouted nets.
- KiCad 10.0.4 exported and XML-parsed all 400 generated schematics.
- All user/internal archive hashes and ZIP integrity checks passed.
- 311 supported native PCBs were accepted; 89 boards were correctly withheld
  by bounded PCB support/routing policy and are not claimed as PCB passes.

The only schematic failure was a single terminal-label layout collision in the
ten `KQ26` profiles. It was not an electrical or parser failure.

## Narrow Corrective Supplement

The root cause was a source pin tip from the `74HC86` multi-unit power section
entering a nearby pin-header body even though the two component bodies did not
overlap. The placement settlement stage now enforces 2.54 mm source-pin to
foreign-body clearance and nudges the smaller movable component first.

The ten affected profiles were regenerated without restarting the 400-case
batch:

```text
kicad/examples/progen_kicad_executable_run_2026_07_17_214207_common400_kq26_pin_clearance_retest_v1/
```

All ten passed every executable stage:

- 10/10 final validation, label-layout, expected-net, and wire-geometry pass.
- 10/10 have zero foreign pin/body conflicts, unresolved pins, partial nets,
  unrouted nets, or PCB unrouted nets.
- 10/10 native PCB outputs were accepted.
- KiCad 10.0.4 exported and XML-parsed every retest schematic.
- All 20 user/internal ZIP artifacts passed integrity checks.

The source-built portable release candidate independently regenerated `KQ26V01`
with the same clean final validation and KiCad 10 netlist export:

```text
kicad/examples/progen_kicad_executable_run_2026_07_17_214428_common400_kq26_portable_rc_smoke_v1/
```

Therefore the qualification evidence is the immutable 390-pass base plus the
fully retested ten-profile corrective supplement. No historical project or
batch result was overwritten.

## Superseded Record

`2026_07_17_common_400_v1` is retained only as an aborted performance record.
Its synthetic 190-component T10 stress archetype was not a practical common
circuit and spent roughly five minutes in bounded PCB routing before a correct
`pcb_routing_limit` withholding decision. It was replaced in v2 by the
practical Arduino/CAN/RS485 motion-controller archetype before v2 testing.
