## Compact Routing Qualification

The readability fix was extended with a hard compactness contract after visual
inspection showed that globally increasing lane numbers could create
sheet-length perimeter loops. The final planner uses per-branch local lanes,
bounded detours, exact terminal keepouts, a second pass for newly discovered
combination-mode terminal fallbacks, and compact terminal banks. The native
validator independently rejects emitted wires outside the bounded component
envelope.

`160..1766 x 160..1934`. The old route envelope reached approximately
`-15000..17500`; the accepted route envelope is `80..1810 x 86..1992`, only
